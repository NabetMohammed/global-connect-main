## 2. Core Activities & Services
- **Import & Export:** Focusing on the promotion of local Moroccan resources, authentic spices, and aromatic herbs, connecting local producers with international markets.
- **Logistics & Road Freight:** Providing reliable freight and transportation solutions to streamline cross-border trade operations.
- **Catering & Cultural Events:** Organizing and managing cultural and artistic festivals dedicated to introducing traditional Moroccan heritage, craftsmanship, culinary arts (hospitality), and the Moroccan Caftan.
- **Social Inclusion & Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR):** Supporting women's entrepreneurship, vulnerable groups, and individuals with disabilities through humanitarian initiatives and institutional partnerships.
